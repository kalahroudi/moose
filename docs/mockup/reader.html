moose — Reader mockup (v1)

The email view, rebuilt around three ideas: brief first (an agent tells you what this is and what it wants from you), clean details on demand (whitespace, tracking URLs and footers tamed), and reply always within reach (docked, never below the fold). All names and content below are invented. Static mockup, not the build.

1 · Default: the brief (light)

Opens instantly on the local text, the brief fills in a moment later (cached per message, so re-opening is instant). Nothing to scroll: summary, what is asked of you, dates and links as chips, one-tap quick replies, and the reply bar is docked at the bottom.

Decisions & how it works

Cleaning (local, instant)Collapse runs of blank lines to one; re-join lines the HTML stripper split mid-sentence (single newline between lowercase fragments becomes a space); long or tracking URLs become a labelled chip showing only the domain, full URL on hover and copy; fold footer boilerplate (unsubscribe, "view in browser", legal, signatures). Raw toggle always available.
Brief (agent, cached)Runs through your own Claude Code CLI like drafting does, only when you open a message, never in bulk. Returns: one-line summary, up to 3 points, who asks what of you, dates, links, "reply needed", urgency. Cached by message id on this Mac, so re-opening is free. If no agent is installed the local summary (first sentence plus detected dates and links) shows instead.
Reply dockAlways pinned. Collapsed bar → docked composer (R) → optional wide split on big windows. Draft is saved as you type, as today. Quote references from selection; Reply all, tones and Draft with Claude stay. Esc collapses without losing the draft.
DictationFloating pill using the system speech engine, on-device where available. Needs microphone and speech permission, requested on first use with a plain explanation. Optional "Tidy with Claude" keeps your meaning and fixes punctuation.
SpeedOpen shows cleaned local text in the same frame, brief streams in after. No web views, no images. Brief and cleaned text are computed once and cached, and the dock is a fixed-height bar so scrolling never reflows.
Agents (MCP)Every new feature is exposed over MCP: get_brief (cached brief for a message), get_clean_text, and draft_reply gains quote (the referenced passage). Sends still need your approval.
Decisions
  • Brief position: full size on open; collapses to a one-line strip once details are open (click to re-expand). The text gets the room.
  • When to brief: only for messages over about 60 words or 5 cleaned lines, generated on open and cached. Short messages show the text directly, with a "Brief" button to force one.
  • Dictation: on-device system dictation only. Instant, free, no model download. "Tidy with Claude" covers cleanup. A Whisper option can come later if needed.
