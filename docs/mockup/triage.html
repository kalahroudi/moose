Rules of this view

  • One card at a time, best first. ← → (or J / K) to move; the only chrome is the counter.
  • Read opens the full text. Reply opens a draft under the message (Claude writes it, you edit; ⌘↩ sends, 5 s undo). Delete moves it to Gmail's Trash, so it's recoverable for 30 days, and the next card appears. ⌘Z undoes the last delete.
  • No scores, tags, summaries, sidebar, mute or star here. Those stay in the main window; this is a separate, quiet window.
  • All ▾ (key A) is the one mass-action control. Pick the scope, This sender or Whole stack, with the count shown, then Archive all, Mark all read or Delete all. The shortcuts ⇧E, ⇧U and ⇧⌫ act on the chosen scope straight away (it defaults to this sender).
  • No “are you sure?” dialogs. Every action runs at once and shows Archived 2 from Stripe · Undo for 10 seconds; ⌘Z restores them. Delete all is still Gmail’s Trash, so it stays recoverable for 30 days.
  • Opens with ⌘T, or from "Open stack" in the menu bar popover.
